Paris, France

About the Place

Paris, the capital of France, is a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Located along the Seine River, it is famously known as the "City of Light."

Many people visit Paris to experience its iconic landmarks, world-class museums, charming cafes, and rich historical architecture spanning centuries.

Top Attractions

  • Eiffel Tower
  • Louvre Museum
  • Cathédrale Notre-Dame de Paris
  • Arc de Triomphe & Champs-Élysées
  • Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur Basilica
  • Seine River Cruise

Travel Tips

Buy museum tickets and Eiffel Tower passes online in advance to skip long lines.
Use the Metro (subway system) for fast and easy transport across the city.
Beware of pickpockets in crowded tourist areas and major train stations.

Quick Facts

Fact Details
Best Season April to June and September to November (mild weather, fewer crowds)
Currency Euro (EUR)
Language French (English is spoken in tourist areas)
Average Cost Moderate to High (depends on accommodation and dining choices)

3-Day Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Historic Landmarks

    Visit the Eiffel Tower in the morning, walk along the Seine River, and take an evening river cruise to see the city lights.

  2. Day 2: Art and Culture

    Explore the iconic Louvre Museum, then take a stroll down the Champs-Élysées to the Arc de Triomphe.

  3. Day 3: Montmartre and Local Vibe

    Wander through the artistic streets of Montmartre, visit the Sacré-Cœur Basilica, and enjoy lunch at a traditional Parisian cafe.
