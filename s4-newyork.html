New York City, USA

About the Place

New York City is the largest city in the United States. It is located on the northeast coast, where the Hudson River meets the Atlantic Ocean.

Many people call New York the city that never sleeps because it is full of tall buildings, theaters, museums, and busy streets day and night.

Top Attractions

  • Statue of Liberty
  • Times Square
  • Central Park
  • Empire State Building
  • Brooklyn Bridge
  • Metropolitan Museum of Art

Travel Tips

Use the subway to move around the city because it is faster than taxis in traffic.
Wear comfortable shoes because you will walk a lot.
Book tickets for popular places like the Statue of Liberty ahead of time.

Quick Facts

Fact Details
Best Season April to June and September to November (mild weather)
Currency US Dollar (USD)
Language English
Average Cost Expensive (check current prices before you go)

3-Day Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Famous Landmarks

    Take a ferry to the Statue of Liberty in the morning, then visit the Empire State Building and see Times Square at night.

  2. Day 2: Parks and Museums

    Walk through Central Park, then spend the afternoon at the Metropolitan Museum of Art.

  3. Day 3: Bridges and Neighborhoods

    Walk across the Brooklyn Bridge, explore the neighborhoods nearby, and buy souvenirs.
