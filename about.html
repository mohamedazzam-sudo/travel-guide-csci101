About Us

We are first-year students at Nile University, and this website is our first HTML project for CSCI101.

About the Project

Our idea is a Simple Travel Destinations Guide. Each page introduces one place, with its top attractions, quick facts, and a short travel plan.

The website has 10 pages: the Home page, the About page, and 8 destination pages. Each team member created 2 destination pages.
