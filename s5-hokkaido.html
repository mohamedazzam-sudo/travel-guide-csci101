Hokkaido, Japan

About the Place

Hokkaido is the northernmost main island of Japan. It is famous for its beautiful nature, mountains, forests, and snowy winters.

Many people visit Hokkaido to enjoy skiing, hot springs, fresh seafood, and beautiful flower fields during the summer.

Top Attractions

  • Sapporo Clock Tower
  • Otaru Canal
  • Furano Flower Fields
  • Niseko Ski Resorts
  • Lake Toya
  • Shiretoko National Park

Travel Tips

Use trains and buses to travel between major destinations because Hokkaido is a large island.
Bring warm clothes during winter because temperatures can drop very low.
Try local seafood and book ski resorts or hot springs ahead of time during busy seasons.

Quick Facts

Fact Details
Best Season December to February for snow and June to August for flowers
Currency Japanese Yen (JPY)
Language Japanese
Average Cost Moderate to expensive (check current prices before you go)

3-Day Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Exploring Sapporo

    Visit the Sapporo Clock Tower in the morning, explore Odori Park, and enjoy a bowl of local ramen in the evening.

  2. Day 2: Otaru and the Coast

    Take a trip to Otaru Canal, explore the charming streets, and try fresh seafood at a local restaurant.

  3. Day 3: Nature and Hot Springs

    Visit Lake Toya, enjoy the beautiful mountain scenery, and relax in a traditional Japanese hot spring.
