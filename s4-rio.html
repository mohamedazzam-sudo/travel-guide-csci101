Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

About the Place

Rio de Janeiro is a large city on the southeast coast of Brazil. It sits between the Atlantic Ocean and green mountains.

Many people call Rio the marvelous city because it has famous beaches, music, and stunning views from the hills.

Top Attractions

  • Christ the Redeemer
  • Sugarloaf Mountain
  • Copacabana Beach
  • Ipanema Beach
  • Selarón Steps
  • Tijuca National Park

Travel Tips

Visit Christ the Redeemer early in the morning to avoid the clouds and the crowds.
Wear sunscreen, a hat, and light clothes, and bring water.
Keep your phone and bag safe, especially on the beach and in crowded places.

Quick Facts

Fact Details
Best Season May to October (cooler and drier weather)
Currency Brazilian Real (BRL)
Language Portuguese (English is spoken in tourist areas)
Average Cost Moderate (check current prices before you go)

3-Day Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Famous Views

    Visit Christ the Redeemer in the morning, then go up Sugarloaf Mountain to watch the sunset.

  2. Day 2: Beaches

    Spend the day at Copacabana Beach and Ipanema Beach, and enjoy fresh coconut water by the sea.

  3. Day 3: Culture and Nature

    Walk up the colorful Selarón Steps, then explore Tijuca National Park and buy souvenirs.
