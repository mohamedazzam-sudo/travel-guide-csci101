Milan, Italy

About the Place

Milan is a major city in northern Italy. It is famous for its fashion, business, art, architecture, and rich cultural history.

Many people visit Milan to admire its historic buildings, explore shopping streets, enjoy Italian food, and discover world-famous art.

Top Attractions

  • Milan Cathedral (Duomo di Milano)
  • Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II
  • Sforza Castle
  • Santa Maria delle Grazie
  • La Scala Opera House
  • Sempione Park

Travel Tips

Use the metro and trams to move around the city because they are convenient for reaching major attractions.
Wear comfortable shoes because many historic areas are best explored on foot.
Book tickets for popular attractions like The Last Supper well in advance because availability can be limited.

Quick Facts

Fact Details
Best Season April to June and September to October (pleasant weather)
Currency Euro (EUR)
Language Italian
Average Cost Moderate to expensive (check current prices before you go)

3-Day Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Famous Landmarks

    Visit Milan Cathedral in the morning, explore Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, and enjoy dinner at a traditional Italian restaurant.

  2. Day 2: Art and History

    Visit Santa Maria delle Grazie to see The Last Supper if you have booked tickets, then explore Sforza Castle and its museums.

  3. Day 3: Shopping and Relaxation

    Explore Milan's famous fashion streets, walk through Sempione Park, and enjoy an Italian coffee before leaving.
